## Alternatives considered
A1 Manual header handling in each endpoint — duplicates Bearer, X-API-Key and the fenced capture seven times, and again in #5.
A2 `HttpSend` interceptor — wraps the whole send pipeline incl. redirects; `createClientPlugin` onRequest/onResponse is the smaller documented surface for header in/out.
A3 Hand-written `KSerializer` per model — re-implements every field per class; one generic `JsonTransformingSerializer` over the generated serializer covers all five.
A4 Atomic epoch via atomicfu/`AtomicLong` CAS — CAS on the epoch cannot make "check epoch then write token" atomic against `clear()`, and Settings has no CAS; a Mutex covers both with no new dependency.
